01 About me

I eliminate computational bottlenecks by designing the silicon hardware and algorithms together, from in-memory logic arrays to real-time wearable telemetry.

I am a final-year undergraduate of Electrical, Electronic and Communication Engineering at the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka, graduating in 2027 with a CGPA of 3.92 / 4.00.

My published and ongoing research spans a 6T SRAM compute-in-memory array for binary neural networks simulated in 45 nm CMOS, FormSense (a wrist-worn 6-DOF IMU pipeline that grades exercise form and detects intra-set neuromuscular fatigue), and optical biosensors in COMSOL.

Industrial attachments at Neural Semiconductor Limited (digital IC design, CMOS cell layout, memory nonidealities) and Walton Hi-Tech Industries PLC. (high-volume automated SMT/PCB assembly, AOI, and quality assurance) provided rigorous insight into bridging circuit simulation with manufacturable hardware.
